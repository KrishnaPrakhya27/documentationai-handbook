---
title: SSO Login Failure
description: Diagnose and fix SSO and SAML login failures with Okta or other identity providers by checking callback URLs, claims, certificates, and provisioning.
---

## Symptoms

- The login redirect fails before authentication completes.

- The platform reports an invalid SAML response.

- The browser remains stuck on a loading screen.

- Okta reports a successful authentication, but the platform displays an error.

## Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the ACS URL" icon="link" title-type="p">

    Check that the Assertion Consumer Service URL configured in your identity provider matches the callback URL expected by the platform.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the Entity ID" icon="fingerprint" title-type="p">

    Confirm that the SAML entity ID, also called the audience URI, matches on both the identity provider and platform configurations.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate the certificate" icon="shield" title-type="p">

    Ensure the identity provider signing certificate is current and uploaded correctly in the platform's SSO settings.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm user provisioning" icon="users" title-type="p">

    Verify that the user exists in both the identity provider and the platform. Check that the email and name claims map to the platform's expected attributes.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Test in an incognito window" icon="eye-off" title-type="p">

    Open an incognito window and retry the login to rule out stale browser sessions and cached SAML cookies.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

SAML attribute mismatches between Okta and the platform are the most common cause of login failures. Check the NameID format and email attribute mapping first.

</Callout>

## Still not working?

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Okta shows a successful login but the app fails">

    The SAML response may be reaching the platform, but its attributes do not match the claims the platform expects. Check the Okta profile attributes for `email`, `firstName`, and `lastName`, then confirm that each attribute maps to the corresponding platform claim.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Login works for some users but not others">

    Check for provisioning gaps. The affected user may not be assigned to the Okta application, or their profile may be missing one or more required attributes.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
