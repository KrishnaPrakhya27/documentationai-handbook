Adding your brand’s identity to documentation is a great way to make your docs site feel like part of your product. And GitBook has plenty of tools to help you do just that. 

Here’s how to utilize a few of them to build a docs site that looks great and fits with your brand.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-4afef969-21f9-40f3-b5b8-b06c8b6cf68f/doc-22a3b060-9539-4176-97e4-49120e02f5d1/b6fb3b81b6b3c664-spaces-LBGJKQic7BQYBXmVSjy0-uploads-UqDwUVWrSWZxP1tqPOEp-CleanShot-2024-12-12-at.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Creating customized docs that match your brand is easy with GitBook." />

### Set a custom domain for your docs

You can [add a custom domain to your site](/en/docs/publish/custom-domain) to make it part of your brand property online. 

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open site settings" titleType="h3">
    Navigate to your docs site in GitBook, then click **Settings** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a custom domain" titleType="h3">
    Choose the **Domain and redirects** tab. Here, you’ll see an option to **set up a custom domain**. Click it and it'll start the process.

    <Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-4afef969-21f9-40f3-b5b8-b06c8b6cf68f/doc-22a3b060-9539-4176-97e4-49120e02f5d1/a6d0a23b4932622f-spaces-LBGJKQic7BQYBXmVSjy0-uploads-ESXE7g3OwKlvNkDx8cFF-set-custom-domain.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Set up a custom domain." />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the setup process" titleType="h3">
    The menu will guide you through the setup process — if you need help, [our documentation](/en/docs/publish/custom-domain) will walk you through the entire process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Customize logo, colors, links and more

Set up your documentation to match your brand by [customizing](#customize-logo-colors-links-and-more) colors, uploading a logo, adding links to your site and much more. Here are a few options to try:

#### Add a custom logo

You can add your company logo to your site — it will sit in the top-left of your site, replacing the site title and icon.

To do this, head to your site dashboard and click **Customization** in the top bar. In the **General** tab you’ll see the option to add a custom logo — click the image preview to upload an image for light mode and dark mode.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-4afef969-21f9-40f3-b5b8-b06c8b6cf68f/doc-22a3b060-9539-4176-97e4-49120e02f5d1/4ce5de9a0214b139-spaces-LBGJKQic7BQYBXmVSjy0-uploads-mpNPymQYMCZ6SKDEBTLG-site-logo.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="You can add custom logos for both light and dark mode, so your docs look great however they’re viewed." />

#### Set a theme and choose colors

Under the same customization panel, scroll down to the **Themes** section. Here, you’ll find settings for changing your site’s colors to match your brand:

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-4afef969-21f9-40f3-b5b8-b06c8b6cf68f/doc-22a3b060-9539-4176-97e4-49120e02f5d1/dbfe732f6bab5645-spaces-LBGJKQic7BQYBXmVSjy0-uploads-4sUNysSLbQSIZb3lrdGB-customize-colors.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="You can set a theme and choose color options from the Themes section — including the Custom theme, which offers more control." />

The first three options let you set a primary color only. Select the **Custom** theme and you’ll also be able to set specific colors for **Header links** and **Header background**, giving you extra control.

Make sure to check your dark mode colors here too, so you can be sure you docs look great in either mode.

#### Change your font and icon style

We have a number of font options you can choose from under the **Styling** section of the **General** tab. Choose from the drop-down. 

In your GitBook space, you can also [add an icon to each page](/en/docs/create-content/content-structure/page#add-an-icon-or-emoji-to-your-page) of your documentation, to add some visual flair and signpost to your readers what you page is about. In your site’s **Customization** menu, you can then tweak the icons’ style using the **Icons** menu. You can make them solid, duotone and more — the choice is yours!

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-4afef969-21f9-40f3-b5b8-b06c8b6cf68f/doc-22a3b060-9539-4176-97e4-49120e02f5d1/ba9b5ff5bef321c8-spaces-LBGJKQic7BQYBXmVSjy0-uploads-7CFlNpUXv7CyT5MsohEJ-customize-styling.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Change your font and icon styles in the Styling section." />

#### Add header links and a footer

Want to add some links to other places — such as your product, a sign-up page or an external source? In the **Layout** tab you can add [header links and buttons](/en/docs/manage-your-site/customization#layout-manage-navigation-options-for-your-content) that will appear at the top of your page, and a footer with a logo, copyright notice and other links, such as a to terms of service page.

You can nest header links if you want to create a drop-down menu in your header, or assign primary and secondary button styling if you want a link to be more prominent.

<Image src="https://test-dai.imgix.net/org-4afef969-21f9-40f3-b5b8-b06c8b6cf68f/doc-22a3b060-9539-4176-97e4-49120e02f5d1/7fcb5310168bbb37-spaces-LBGJKQic7BQYBXmVSjy0-uploads-UOezObbdgvkGx7UdU8ch-customize-header-footer.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="In the Layout tab, you can add links and buttons to your header, and create a footer with your company logo, copyright information, and links." />
