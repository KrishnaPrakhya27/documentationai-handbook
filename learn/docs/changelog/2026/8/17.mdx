## Standalone Ask AI sidebar panel

<span className="dai-mig-badge">ai, search</span>

The [`@fern-api/search-widget`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fern-api/search-widget) package now ships an `AskAiChat` component: a sidebar panel that answers questions from your documentation inside any React application, like a dashboard or marketing site. The panel becomes a bottom drawer on small screens, and the `theme` and `accentColor` props match it to your product.
